# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Subway IRL to be a friendly, welcoming place to play, learn and build, whatever your age, background,
experience, body or ability. The game is played with your body, so we especially want everyone to feel comfortable
moving at their own pace.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully. Critique the code or the idea, never the person.
- Respect different play spaces, abilities and hardware.
- Keep discussion on topic and free of harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling or spam.
- Do not share other people's private information.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment of any kind, hateful or sexualised language or imagery, threats, doxxing, and any behaviour that would be
unwelcome in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this
code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone who breaks it.

To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" on the comment or open a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/Yashism/Subway-IRL/security/advisories/new) addressed to the maintainer.
Reports are handled privately.

This code is adapted from the ideas in the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
